Drop a file called  logo.png  in this folder.

That is all it takes — the nav and the footer pick it up on the next reload,
at whatever aspect ratio it has, and the drawn placeholder badge disappears.

  public/logo.png

Square or round artwork fits the slot best (it is rendered at 120px in the nav
and 180px in the footer, scaled to fit, never cropped). A wide lockup works too:
it will be limited to 540px across in the nav rather than squashed.

Export at 3x the display size — 540px square is plenty — and keep the
background transparent, because the page behind it is white.

Until the file exists, the site draws its own badge instead. Nothing breaks
while the slot is empty.
